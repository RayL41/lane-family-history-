THE IRISH CENSUS FOR 1901 SHRAVOKEE

(SRAWICKEEN)

We know that the following Lane Families occupied land in the old Town Land of Shravokee at the time of the Griffiths Valuation in 1855.


Thomas Lane (Moloney) - 38 Acres
Thomas Lane (Mulqueeny) - 33 Acres
James Lane  - 23 Acres
Patrick Lane - 31 Acres

Working back from records we can trace the family of our grandfather Michael Lane b1841 who arrived in Australia and farmed at Koroit, in Victoria, back to Shravokee to his Grandfather Michael Lane b1788.

Michael Lane b1841 married Ellen Moloney.
Thomas Lane b1818 d1891 married Elizabeth Gleeson.
Michael Lane b1788 married Catherine Moloney.

We do know that descendents of this Michael Lane b1788 still  farm at Shravokee on the property listed in the Griffiths Valuation.

We do not know yet the actual relationship between Michael Lane b1788 and the Lanes listed in the Griffiths Valuation.

At the time of the 1901 Census in Ireland there were 6 families living in the Town Land of Shravokee (Srawickeen) of which three were Lane Families. The other families being those of John Madden, Patrick Cusack and Bridget Madden.

The three Lane family groups were descended from the following families who held land at Shravokee in some way at time of the Griffiths Valuation in 1855 and would be descended via Thomas Lane (Moloney) or Thomas Lane (Mulqueeny).

1. Michael Lane (b1788) and Catherine Moloney.

2. James Lane (b1810) and Ann Malone

3. Thomas Lane (b1805) and Ellen Moloney

There is a headstone in the Lane Cemetery at Teampail near the 1855 Lane farms listed in the Griffiths Valuation.


This headstone links the Lanes who held land in the
Griffiths Valuation of 1855.
It reads -:

ERECTED BY THOMAS, PATRICK & ANNE LANE
and their COUSIN
THOMAS LANE
in memory of
JAMES LANE
who departed this life
10th February 1855
aged 45 years


The Thomas Lane's listed on the Headstone are believed to be Thomas Lane (Mulqueeney) and Thomas Lane (Moloney) who both occupied farms at Shravokee and Mt. O'Donnell in the Griffiths Valuation of 1855.
Patrick Lane is believed to be Patrick Lane from Quinpool.
 
The James Lane on the Headstone, occupied land at Mt O'Donnell (near Shravokee) and was married to Anne Malone. She is the other name on the Headstone.
The Malone family occupied land at Shravokee.

The holding of James Lane in the Griffiths Valuation of 1855 was previously held by Thomas Lane (Mulqueeny).
It is the property at Mt. O'Donnell at the time of the Tithe in 1824 and The Freeholders of 1829 and shown in the possession of Thomas Lane (Mulqueeny). 
It is believed that James Lane was a son of Thomas Lane (Mulqueeny) 

After the death of James Lane the land was transferred to his widow Anne Lane (Malone).

  It Is understood that this is the property listed as House 8, shown below, in the 1901 Census for Srawickeen (Cappavilla, Clare) and at the time of the Tithe was noted as Mt. O'Donnell.

Margaret Lane & Mary Anne Lane who came to Australia and married the Gleeson Brothers were Grandchildren of James Lane and Anne Malone..

The holdings Held by Thomas Lane (Moloney) and Thomas Lane (Mulqueeny) are referred to in the The Tithe Applotment Records of 1824, The Index to Freeholders of 1829 as well as The Griffiths Valuation of 1855.
The use of the names Moloney and Mulqueeny after the name Thomas Lane was to differentiate between the two landholders and was possibly the wives maiden name. 

By using the Census of 1901 and the Census of 1911 we can show the families that descend from both Thomas Lane (Moloney) and Thomas Lane (Mulqueeny)

Thomas Lane (Moloney) Descendants

This property we believe is the original Thomas Lane (Moloney) property of 38 acres shown in The Tithe report of 1824 and the Griffiths Valuation of 1855 and was inherited by Thomas Lane b1839.
Thomas was the son of Thomas Lane (born1818) and Elizabeth Gleeson.
Descendants of the Madden and Lane families still farm there today.

This is the Family of the Thomas Lane b1839, who married Mary Ward of Castleconnell on 13th January, 1870.
He was the son of Thomas Lane b1818 and Elizabeth Gleeson and the Grandson of Michael Lane b1788 and Catherine Moloney from whom we are descended.
He was a brother to our Michael Lane, b1841 who came to Australia and who married Ellen Moloney..

Thomas Lane b1839 died before the Census on 25 August 1895 age 56, from a lung condition (Hemoptysis).
Probate was granted to his wife Mary Lane (Ward)

Note 1: Maggie Maloneys is shown in the 1911 census as Margaret Moloney, relative.
She was blind in both census and was a cousin of Thomas Lane b1818 and brought to Shravokee by his wife Elizabeth Gleesom.

Note 2: Willie Lane is listed in the 1911 census as William Lane.
He emigrated to the USA and he married Joanna Spaight in the USA..

Note 3: Mary Lane (Ward) is shown in the 1911 census as being married 40 years with 6 children and 4 still alive.
The 1911 Census indicates she was born in Co. Tipperary but she was actually came from Castleconnell, Co. Clare.
Living with Mary at the time of the 1811 census were John aged 29, Mary 27,Michael 25, William 23 as well as Margaret Moloney 72 - a cousin of Thomas Lane b1818 and who was blind.
 Mary Lane (Ward) the widow of Thomas Lane died at Shravokee in February, 1923 aged 80. The informant was her son John Lane of Shravokee.

Note 4: John Lane was baptised 4th April, 1879 and married Ellen McMahon.
 Click here for Photo of John Lane & Ellen McMahon taken at the front of the old house at Shravokee.
Their daughter Patricia Lane b1926 married John Madden.
Patricia Lane's brothers included Father Tom Lane and Father Michael Lane. 

Note 5: Michael Lane was born 12 November 1881 and died 2 Aug 1919 age 37 of TB.
He was a bachelor farmer. His brother John was the informant.

Note 6: Mary Lane, the daughter, also known as Babe, was born 8 May 1875.
She never married and remained on the farm and died in 1947 aged 71.
HOUSE DETAIL.
In the 1901 Census the house was a Class 2 house, brick construction, with a thatched roof, 4 rooms with 3 windows in the front.
The farm had a stable, a cow house, piggery and a barn.
The Farms in the 1901 census at Shravokee were occupied by (1) Patrick Dillon (John Lane), (2) John Madden, (3) Pat Madden- land only, (4)Patrick Cusack, (5) Mary Lane (Ward), (7) Patrick Lane- land only, (7) Bridget Mulqueen and (8) Mary Anne Lane (Keane)

In the 1911 Census the property was designated as House 7 which was larger, a Class 2 house, brick construction, with an iron or slate roof, with 7 rooms and 5 windows in the front.
It had a stable, a cow house, a calf house, a piggery, a boiling house and a barn.
This was Shravokee House.
The farms in the Census of 1911 at Shravokee were occupied by (1) Jeremiah Ryan, (2) James Sullivan , (3) John Madden, (4) Bridget Mulqueen, (5) Mary Anne Kane (Keane), (6) Daniel Madden, (7) Mary Lane (Ward) and (8) John Lane (Dillon).

 Of the children Jim, Mary and Annie did not marry and remained at Shravokee for many years..

By the time of the 1911 Census Margaret Lane had left for the USA and Bridget Lane (Delia) immigrated to the USA not long after.
Margaret Lane married Martin Madden. She was born September25, 1980.
Delia Lane married a Dan Clancy. They did not have any family. She was born October 21, 1884.

Bridget (Delia) was shown as 23 years old at the time of the 1911 census.
She was a passenger on the ss Cedric which arrived in New York in 1912, aged 24 and stated her mother was Mary Lane from Shravokee, Clonlara, County Clare and she was going to meet her sister Maggie Lane in Brooklyn.
