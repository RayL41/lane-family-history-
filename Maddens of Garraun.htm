Srawickween (Shravokee) and Garraun were adjoining townlands in County Clare near the County Limerick border.

Land held at the time of the Tithe Applotment in 1824.

At the time of the Tithe Applotment in 1824 the Maddens held land in Gurrane (Garraun) and the Lanes held lane in Mt Donnell (noted as part of Srawickeen).
Daniel Madden and James Madden held 13 acres of land with Thomas Arthur, John McCormack, Michael & John Malone and the Widow Greany.
Daniel Madden and John Madden held 27acres of land plus an area of bog land.
James Madden had a further of 2 acres of land.

At Mt Donnell.
Thomas Lane held 17acres of land with Pat Mulqueeny and John Mulqueeny.

At Isleland.
Thomas Lane also held land with Roger Moloney and Daniel Shaughnessy.

 Land held at the time of the Griffiths Valuation in 1855.

The Lane Family of Shravokee and the Madden family of Garraun had adjacent properties at the time of the Griffith Valuation in 1855.

In Garraun the following properties were occupied by Madden families.
Property Ref 2a,b,c,d on the Griffiths Map was occupied by Daniel Madden - 26 acres house, offices and land.
Property 5 was occupied by Daniel Madden, John Malone and Patrick Casey - 8 acres land.
Property 6 was occupied by John Madden and Michael Madden - 14 acres land.
Property 7 was occupied by Michael Madden - 4 acres land.
Property 8 was occupied by Michael Madden and Patrick Lane - 21 acres land.

Also of note is property 8a next to property 8 which was listed as a  graveyard.

In Srawickeen (Shravokee) the following properties were occupied by Lane families.
Property Ref 3a was occupied by Thomas Lane (Moloney), John Shaughnessy, Bridget Malone, Thos. Lane (Mulqueeny)  and Michael Moloney - 38 acres house,offices and land.
Ref 5a, b. was occupied by Thos. Lane (Mulqueeny) - 30 acres land.
Ref 11a, was occupied by James Lane - 23 acres house, offices and lane.

It is in this graveyard that many Lanes, Maddens and other names listed in the area are buried
