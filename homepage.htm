From the arrival of the First Fleet

to Australia in 1788

to the New Millenium.

Hi, welcome to the genealogical web site of Ray Lane.

This site deals with the history of the many branches of our family and includes Edward Whitton, who arrived from England as a convict on the First Fleet in 1788, to Michael Lane, the first of our immediate Lane family to arrive from Ireland in 1861.

If you have a connection with any of these families , I would be very happy to hear from you.

Should you find any errors or ommissions please let me know and I will gladly change them.
